# Jurica Seva - AI Leadership Consulting Brand Bundle

This bundle is designed for fractional, interim and project-based leadership engagements in AI, data and software engineering, especially roles such as:

- Fractional Chief AI Officer / Fractional CTO
- Interim VP / Head of AI or Data
- AI Transformation Lead
- AI Platform / GenAI Strategy Lead
- Director / VP-level technical transformation engagements

## Recommended positioning

**AI Transformation & Engineering Executive**

I help companies turn AI ambition into production systems, operating models and measurable delivery. I work across executive strategy, AI/platform architecture, team design, governance and hands-on technical execution.

Target engagements are scoped as mandates, not hours: a ~2-week AI Transformation Diagnostic (€7,500–12,500), a fractional CAIO / VP AI mandate (1 day/week €7,000–9,000/month; 2 days/week €13,000–16,000/month), or a 3–6 month AI Transformation Mandate (~€20,000–30,000/month), plus VAT where applicable.

## Bundle contents

- `cv/` - leadership CV in DOCX and PDF
- `profiles/executive_profile.md` - one-page executive profile copy
- `profiles/linkedin_profile.md` - LinkedIn headline, About, services and role descriptions
- `sales/capability_statement.md` - client-facing capability statement
- `market/platform_strategy.md` - where to apply and how to position on each platform
- `market/outreach_templates.md` - concise recruiter/client outreach templates
- `website/` - production-oriented Next.js landing site with Insights pages, structured data, sitemap, robots and llms.txt
- `content_plan.md` - 12-week authority-building plan

## Website setup

1. `cd website`
2. `npm install`
3. Replace `https://your-domain.com` in `app/layout.tsx`, `app/sitemap.ts`, and `public/llms.txt` with your production domain.
4. Update the Calendly / booking URL in `app/page.tsx` if you want a direct scheduling CTA.
5. `npm run dev` for local development.
6. Deploy to Vercel, AWS Amplify, Cloudflare (Next.js adapter), or your existing Node/container infrastructure.

The site intentionally uses no UI component framework and almost no dependencies, making it easy to adapt to your existing infrastructure.
