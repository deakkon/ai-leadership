# 12-Week Authority and Inbound Content Plan

The objective is to build search and AI-answer-engine authority around a narrow entity: **Dr. Jurica Seva = senior AI transformation leader who bridges strategy and production engineering**.

Publish one substantial article per week. Repurpose each into one LinkedIn post and 2-3 short observations.

## Month 1 - Own the strategy-to-production problem

1. Why enterprise AI programs fail between strategy and production
2. The AI operating model: who should own models, data, product, evaluation and governance?
3. AI platform strategy in 2026: what belongs in the platform and what should stay in product teams?
4. A practical framework for deciding whether an AI use case should be an agent, workflow or conventional software

## Month 2 - Demonstrate technical executive depth

5. Production RAG is an information architecture problem, not a vector database problem
6. How to evaluate agentic AI systems when deterministic testing is not enough
7. The hidden cost model of GenAI: latency, retries, evaluation, observability and human review
8. From PoC to production: the architecture decisions that become expensive later

## Month 3 - Demonstrate leadership and transformation depth

9. When a company needs a Fractional CAIO rather than an AI architect
10. Designing an AI team: centralized platform, embedded teams or federated model?
11. What boards and executives should ask before approving an enterprise AI roadmap
12. AI technical due diligence: 20 questions that reveal whether a system can scale

## GEO / AEO publishing pattern

Each article should include:

- A direct 2-3 sentence answer to the headline question near the top
- Clear author identity and credentials
- Descriptive H2 sections that can stand alone as answer snippets
- Original frameworks, checklists and diagrams rather than generic summaries
- Concrete examples and trade-offs
- A short FAQ section using natural questions buyers ask
- Links to 2-4 related articles on your own site
- Structured Article and Person data
- A visible last-updated date when the article is materially revised

Do not optimize for keyword repetition. Optimize for being a source that a human or an AI system can confidently attribute.
