# AI Transformation & Engineering Leadership
## Dr. Jurica Seva

### What I solve

Organizations rarely fail at AI because they cannot run a model. They fail because strategy, architecture, data, governance, product ownership and engineering execution are disconnected.

I work with leadership teams that need one accountable senior operator across those boundaries. I can enter at strategy level, define the target state, establish decision criteria and governance, design the technical architecture and operating model, and then work directly with engineering teams through delivery.

### Core engagement areas

**1. Fractional / interim AI leadership**  
Temporary executive ownership for AI, data or AI engineering functions. Roadmap, priorities, stakeholder alignment, team structure, hiring, architecture governance and execution cadence.

**2. AI transformation strategy**  
Portfolio assessment, value hypotheses, build/buy/partner decisions, capability gaps, operating model, governance, investment sequencing and measurable transformation roadmap.

**3. GenAI and agentic systems**  
LLM applications, RAG, tool-using agents, workflow automation, evaluation, reliability, observability, human-in-the-loop controls and production adoption.

**4. AI platform and architecture**  
Production architecture across model access, data/retrieval, orchestration, APIs, cloud, security, observability, evaluation and lifecycle management.

**5. PoC-to-production recovery**  
Technical and organizational review of AI initiatives that work in demos but fail on quality, cost, latency, governance, ownership or integration.

**6. AI organization and engineering quality**  
Team topology, role design, hiring, engineering standards, architecture decision processes, AI evaluation practices, delivery workflows and mentoring.
