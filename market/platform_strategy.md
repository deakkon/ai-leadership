# Platform Strategy for Senior AI Leadership Contracting

Last reviewed: August 2026.

The goal is not maximum profile volume. It is to appear in channels where buyers expect senior independent consultants, interim executives or technical leaders and where mandate-based engagements — a €7,500–12,500 diagnostic, €7,000–16,000/month fractional mandates, or a ~€20,000–30,000/month transformation program — are structurally plausible.

## Tier 1 - Prioritize

### Malt Strategy
https://www.malt.com/c/maltstrategy/consultants

Why: Malt Strategy is specifically positioned as an exclusive community for vetted management consultants and interim executives, rather than the broader freelancer marketplace.

Position as: **Fractional Chief AI Officer / AI Transformation Executive**.

Lead with: VP-level ownership, AI operating model, transformation, executive stakeholder work, regulated delivery, team leadership, and strategy-to-production capability.

### High5 / Talmix
https://www.talmix.com/talent

Why: Focuses on independent business and consulting talent, including project and interim opportunities with global companies, consultancies and PE portfolio companies.

Position as: **AI Transformation Lead / Interim VP AI / AI & Data Operating Partner-style advisor**.

Best angle: transformation programs, PE portfolio value creation, AI capability assessment, platform modernization and execution recovery.

### Catalant
https://catalant.com/

Why: Strong fit for independent strategy and transformation consultants, with explicit Digital & AI work and a focus on embedding experts to turn strategy into implementation.

Position as: **AI Strategy-to-Execution Lead**.

Best angle: a senior operator who can bridge strategy decks and engineering execution.

### A.Team
https://www.a.team/join

Why: Vetted network for senior engineering and AI builders. It explicitly includes engineering leaders and Fractional CTOs, allows talent to set rates, and emphasizes high-impact enterprise missions.

Position as: **Fractional CTO / AI Architect / Engineering & Technical Leader**.

Best angle: use this channel for the most technical version of your leadership profile. Do not over-index on pure strategy here.

## Tier 2 - Strong secondary channels

### Business Talent Group (BTG)
https://businesstalentgroup.com/

Strong for enterprise independent consulting and interim leadership. Their AI offering spans strategy through implementation. The best entry point is a senior independent AI strategist / transformation executive profile rather than an engineering freelancer profile.

### Outvise
https://www.outvise.com/

EMEA-oriented marketplace for high-end Business Tech experts, including management consultants, AI/data experts and digital transformation profiles. Particularly relevant when the buyer is a telecom, technology, enterprise IT or transformation organization.

### Broad Malt marketplace
https://www.malt.com/

Maintain a profile because of European reach, but treat it as a discovery surface rather than your primary positioning channel. Avoid competing on the platform's generic freelancer rate benchmarks. Your profile should look like interim executive / transformation consulting, not commodity AI development.

## Channels I would de-prioritize

Generic volume marketplaces where the dominant buying behavior is low-friction staff augmentation or price comparison. They can produce work, but they make it harder to preserve executive positioning and mandate-based pricing economics.

## Profile title hierarchy

Use one of these depending on channel:

1. **Fractional Chief AI Officer | AI Transformation & Engineering Executive**
2. **Interim VP / Head of AI | GenAI & AI Platform Transformation**
3. **Fractional CTO | AI Platforms, Agentic AI & Engineering Leadership**
4. **AI Transformation Lead | Strategy, Operating Model & Production Delivery**

Avoid leading with "AI Engineer" unless the platform is specifically matching hands-on technical missions. It anchors the buyer to staff-augmentation economics before they understand the leadership value.

## Commercial model

Do not present pricing as one undifferentiated number in every context. Engagements are scoped as mandates, not hours.

- **AI Transformation Diagnostic:** ~2 weeks, €7,500–12,500 one-off. Entry point for a focused AI capability / architecture assessment.
- **Fractional CAIO / VP AI (also Fractional CTO):** 1 day/week €7,000–9,000/month; 2 days/week €13,000–16,000/month. Fractional executive ownership with explicit outcomes, accountability and an exit criterion.
- **AI Transformation Mandate:** 3–6 months, ~€20,000–30,000/month. One accountable operator taking a program from fragmented PoCs into production.

The commercial story should be about reducing executive and delivery risk, not selling hours.
