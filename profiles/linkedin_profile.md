## About

I help companies turn AI ambition into production systems, operating models and measurable execution.

My work combines three layers that are too often separated: executive AI strategy, engineering leadership and hands-on technical architecture. I work with founders, C-level leaders and engineering teams to decide where AI creates real value, design the platform and operating model, build the right team, and move initiatives from experimentation into reliable production use.

I have 10+ years of experience across applied AI, NLP, data platforms, search and software systems. As VP of AI & Data Science at Adia Health, I owned the AI platform roadmap and architecture, built and led an 8-person cross-timezone AI/data organization, and led GenAI, clinical NLP, knowledge-graph and semantic-search delivery in a regulated AWS environment.

My recent work includes fractional AI leadership, GenAI strategy, agentic systems, RAG, AI evaluation and reliability, workflow automation, AI governance, platform architecture, hiring and organization design.

I am most useful when a company needs someone who can work at leadership level without losing technical depth: shaping the strategy, challenging architecture choices, translating between business and engineering, and helping teams actually ship.

- AI transformation lead
- GenAI and agentic-AI strategy
- AI platform and architecture advisory
- AI capability assessment and technical due diligence
